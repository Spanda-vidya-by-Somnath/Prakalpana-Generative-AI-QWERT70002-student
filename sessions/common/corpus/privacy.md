# Your account and privacy

We store your name, phone number, addresses, order history and payment tokens (never full card numbers).
Order history is kept for 3 years for tax reasons. You can download your data or delete your account in
Settings → Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days; open orders and wallet balances must be settled first.

We never sell personal data. We share your phone number with the rider only for the duration of a delivery,
masked through a call bridge. Marketing messages are opt-in and can be switched off per channel.
