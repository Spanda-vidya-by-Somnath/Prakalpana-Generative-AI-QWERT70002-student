# Delivery areas and times

## Where we deliver
- **Pune:** Baner, Aundh, Balewadi, Wakad, Pashan, Kothrud, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Viman Nagar.
- **Bengaluru:** Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, Domlur, Ejipura, BTM Layout, Bellandur.
Check a pin code in the app before ordering; we add new areas every quarter.

## Delivery slots
| Slot type | Window | Available |
|---|---|---|
| Express | within 30 minutes | 7 am – 10 pm, orders up to 25 items |
| Scheduled | 2-hour slots | 7 am – 11 pm, book up to 3 days ahead |
| Early morning | 6 am – 8 am | milk, bread, eggs and fruit only; order by 10 pm the night before |

Express delivery is paused during heavy rain alerts issued by the IMD; scheduled slots continue.
If we are more than 15 minutes late on an Express order, the delivery fee is refunded automatically.
