# Payments and Monsoon Wallet

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, and cash on delivery.
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹3,000 and is not available for Express orders after 9 pm.

**Monsoon Wallet:** refunds and cashback land here. Wallet balance never expires, but promotional credits expire
after 60 days. Wallet money can be moved to your bank account once a month, minimum ₹100, at no charge.

**Failed payments:** if money was debited but the order failed, it is reversed automatically within 3 working days.
After 3 days, raise a ticket with the UPI reference number.
