# Out-of-stock items and substitutions

When you order, choose one option per item:
- **Substitute:** we pick the closest item of the same or larger size and same or lower price per unit. You can reject
  it at the door and are not charged.
- **Call me:** the picker calls you; if you don't answer within 2 minutes, the item is skipped.
- **Skip:** the item is removed and not charged.

The default is "Call me". We never substitute baby food, medicines or items you have marked as allergy-sensitive.
