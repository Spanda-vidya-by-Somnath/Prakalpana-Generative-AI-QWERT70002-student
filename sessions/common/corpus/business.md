# Bulk and business orders

Offices, restaurants and housing societies can open a Monsoon Business account. Requirements: GST number and a
minimum monthly spend of ₹25,000. Benefits: GST invoices, 30-day credit after 3 months of on-time payments, a named
account manager, and next-morning delivery between 7 am and 10 am.

Orders above 200 items or ₹50,000 must be placed 24 hours ahead. Business accounts are not eligible for consumer
coupons or Plus benefits. Contact business@monsoonmart.example to apply.
