# Monsoon Plus membership

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | ₹149 per month | cancel any time |
| Yearly | ₹1,199 per year | about ₹100 per month |
| Student | ₹79 per month | valid college ID, verified once a year |

**Benefits:** free delivery and no Express surcharge on orders above ₹99, no night fee, 5% cashback in Monsoon Wallet
on fresh produce (capped at ₹300 per month), early access to sales, and a dedicated support line.

**Cancelling:** monthly plans stop at the end of the paid month; no refunds for part months.
Yearly plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase are refunded in full if no benefit was used; after that, no refund.
A free 30-day trial is offered once per account.
