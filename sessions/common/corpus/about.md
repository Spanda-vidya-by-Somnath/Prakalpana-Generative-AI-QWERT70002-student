# About Monsoon Mart

Monsoon Mart is an online grocery delivery service founded in 2021 in Pune by Asha Kulkarni and Ravi Menon.
It now serves Pune and Bengaluru from four dark stores: Baner and Hadapsar in Pune, Indiranagar and HSR Layout in Bengaluru.

We stock about 6,500 products: fresh fruit and vegetables, dairy, staples, snacks, household and personal care.
Around 40% of our fresh produce comes directly from farmer producer organisations within 150 km of each city.

Customer support runs from 7 am to 11 pm every day, in English, Hindi, Marathi and Kannada.
Our head office is at 4th Floor, Sakal Nagar Tech Park, Baner, Pune 411045.
