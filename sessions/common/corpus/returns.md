# Returns and refunds (current policy, effective 1 April 2026)

## Fresh produce, dairy and bakery
Report a problem within **48 hours** of delivery with a photo in the app. No need to return the item.
Refunds go to your Monsoon Wallet instantly, or to the original payment method in 5–7 working days if you choose.

## Packaged and household items
Unopened items can be returned within **10 days** of delivery. Our rider collects them on your next order or on a
free pickup you book in the app. Opened items can be returned only if damaged or expired on arrival.

## Items that cannot be returned
Baby food and formula once opened, medicines, gift cards, and items marked "final sale".

## Refund amounts
Refunds include the item price and any delivery fee for that order if the whole order is returned.
Coupons used on a refunded order are restored to your account if still valid.
