# Offers and coupons

- **First order:** 20% off up to ₹150 with code `NEWMONSOON`, minimum order ₹399. One use per phone number.
- **Referral:** you and your friend each get ₹100 in Monsoon Wallet after their first delivered order above ₹499.
  Maximum 10 referrals a year.

Only one coupon can be used per order. Coupons don't apply to baby formula, gift cards or the Plus membership fee.
Wallet cashback and coupons can be combined.
