# Delivery fees and minimum order

| Order value | Delivery fee |
|---|---|
| Below ₹199 | not accepted (minimum order is ₹199) |
| ₹199 – ₹499 | ₹35 |
| ₹500 – ₹999 | ₹15 |
| ₹1,000 and above | free |

Express delivery adds a ₹20 surcharge on any order value.
A small-cart handling fee does not exist; ignore older app screenshots that show one.
Monsoon Plus members pay no delivery fee and no Express surcharge on orders above ₹99.
Late-night orders (10 pm – 11 pm) carry a ₹10 night fee, also waived for Plus members.
