# Fresh produce quality promise

Fruit and vegetables are picked from our dark stores the same day they arrive from farms, usually before 6 am.
Every item carries a "best before" date on the bag label. Leafy greens are guaranteed fresh for 2 days, most
other vegetables for 4 days, and fruit by ripeness label: "eat today", "ready in 2 days" or "ready in 4 days".

If produce doesn't last as promised, report it within 48 hours of the end of the guarantee period with a photo
for a full refund. Our quality team reviews every photo and uses them to rate farm partners.
